# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, considerate, and constructive. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, and unwanted disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the [Novejuro GitHub profile](https://github.com/Novejuro). Please include enough context to understand what happened. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used for Nexora project work.
